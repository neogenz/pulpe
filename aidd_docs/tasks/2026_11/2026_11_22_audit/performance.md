# Codebase Audit: « Comparer avec mes comptes » (performance)

The one real cost is the server context read that step 2 waits for: about 17 queries over 10 sequential round trips, most of them redundant. iOS and Android repeat that read after every check, current-month checks included. Client-side recomputation is cheap.

- **Date**: 2026_11_22
- **Scope**: `git diff origin/main` + uncommitted tree of `claude/reconciliation-onboarding-redo` (HEAD `5ed3423a2`, base `b3656f067`): `backend-nest/src/modules/reconciliation/` and the budget read it calls, `shared/src/calculators/account-reconciliation.ts`, `frontend/projects/webapp/src/app/pattern/reconcile-accounts/`, `ios/Pulpe/Features/CurrentMonth/Reconciliation/`, `android/src/features/current-month/` (reconcile*, reconciliation*) and the check invalidation they added in `android/src/features/budgets/toggle-check-mutation.ts`

Checked and not a finding:

- **Client recomputation.** `rankedPointingCandidates` (`shared/src/calculators/account-reconciliation.ts:260-312`) is O(U × (L + T)) per recompute, where U is the unchecked items and L + T are the month's lines and movements. `calculateRealizedBalance` is linear: it indexes movements by line (`budget-formulas.ts:223`), and the Swift twin does the same (`BudgetFormulas.swift`, Dictionary index). That is a few tens of thousands of additions for a typical month. It runs only on step 2, never per keystroke: web in a `computed` gated on the context resource, which is idle outside step 2 (`reconcile-accounts-dialog.ts:228-246`); iOS once per body evaluation (`ReconcileCompareStepView.swift:42`); Android once per render while `step === 2` (`reconcile-accounts-sheet.tsx:234-251`, React Compiler on).
- **Server prior items.** `pointingEffects` in `prior-correction.ts:44-48` uses the same O(U × (L + T)) shape on two months. Negligible CPU next to the round trips above.
- **Rekey table lock.** `LOCK TABLE public.user_reconciliation IN SHARE ROW EXCLUSIVE MODE` (`migration:85` and v1-v3) briefly blocks everyone's state writes while one person changes their PIN. This is intentional and documented in `docs/ENCRYPTION.md`: the row count under the lock must be authoritative, and the row may not exist yet. Rekeys are rare and short. Accepted.
- **Unbounded payloads.** The context returns at most two months of unchecked items, and the PUT body is two fields. Nothing is unpaginated in a way that grows with history, except the rollover list behind the major finding.

## Top actions

1. Rewrite the previous-month read in `GetReconciliationContextUseCase` around one rollover list and two parallel `fetchBudgetData` calls (major row). This removes most of the latency on step 2 and on every check. Hand off to `aidd-dev:07-refactor` or `backend-developer`, with the existing e2e suite as the guard.
2. Limit the context re-read to previous-month items on iOS and Android, as the web does (first minor row). Small, and independent of action 1.
3. Lazy-load the web reconcile dialog from `ReconcileAccountsDialogService.open()`, then remeasure the `current-month` and `budget-details` chunks (second minor row).
