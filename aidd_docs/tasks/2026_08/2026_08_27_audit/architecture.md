# Codebase Audit: android/ (architecture)

The routing contract has one decider (`landingRoute`) but the navigator resolves guard flips on its own, and that gap is the "PIN creation asked again" bug.

- **Date**: 2026-08-27
- **Scope**: `android/` (Expo SDK 57, expo-router 57.0.16, 379 source files)
- **Health**: fair
- **Findings**: 1 critical, 1 warning, 2 minor
